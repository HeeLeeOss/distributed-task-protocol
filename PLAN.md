## Summary

A reusable, documented protocol for coordinating chunked work across many contributors or agents, with built-in verification — generalized from Hee-Lee Oss's own task flow.

## Scope

In scope: a protocol spec, verification model, and reference documentation. Out of scope: a specific hosted implementation.

## Risk

Medium: educational/derivative work on openly-licensed sources with recorded provenance; no personal data and no prescriptive/personalized advice. Each deliverable cites sources and stays within its stated non-advice / non-arbiter / non-partisan bounds.

## Approach

A protocol spec, a verification model, a claim/receipt format, a reference flow, and interop/security notes.

## Definition of shipped

A documented, reusable protocol spec for chunk coordination and verification, adopted by at least one project.
